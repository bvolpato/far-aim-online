# Android Data Safety Notes

FAR/AIM Pro does not require an account and does not collect personal data through an app backend. The Android app ships without analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, or network permissions.

The app stores study state, reading history, theme settings, quiz progress, search history, saved references, highlights, and notes locally on the device. This local data is used only for app functionality and is not sold or shared. Users can export a study pack to a file they choose; the app does not upload it to FAR/AIM Pro servers.

The app bundles FAA reference content and does not need network access for normal content browsing after install. Support and Privacy Policy links open the user's browser.
